Files

  • sea_tss.bin — traffic-separation geometry (header TSS1)
  • sea_zones.bin — sea areas, including the OpenStreetMap Yangtze record (header SZN2)
  • scripts/build_sea_tss.py — Overpass extraction and packer
  • scripts/supplements.py — public-regulation schemes added where OSM had none

UKHO, Admiralty, and MARNET data are not in these files.
